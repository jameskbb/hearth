Desert Twilight decodes to 24,948,480 bytes (23.8 MiB) against the 32 MB budget:
two full-size skies (day and twilight) at 1920x1080, stars and clouds at 960 px
wide, and the mesas as strips (1920x300 from y 230, 1920x592 from y 240).

Weather variants

Provenance: original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG sources; no third-party material.
The mesa-following layers (13, 14, 18, 19) reuse the mesa paths and displacement noise of layers 04 and
05, with the same strip origin, overscan and parallax, so they sit on the drawn tops, ledges and plain
and sway with them. Every variant keeps the day sky, the twilight sky fading in over it, and the
afterglow: weather layers are translucent overlays drawn above both skies, so the crossfade still
happens beneath them. The stars and lenticular clouds are hidden under cloud.

- snow: a lavender-pearl cloud deck (deck) over both skies, broken snow caps on the far mesa tops only where a
  surface faces up (snow-far), snow on the cliff's ledges, a snow-covered plain with irregular bare patches
  (small and close together toward the horizon, larger toward the viewer) lit toward the horizon and lavender
  nearer, rose where the afterglow reaches, filled drifts banked on the dune line and the near ground, all
  feathered out before the strip ends (snow-near), and two
  falling-snow layers (snowfall-far, snowfall-near, 270 dp tiles). Snow on mesas is rare but real.
- snow-coming-frost (forecast snow, freezing): the deck, the pearl-rose hush (hush) and a light dusting
  (snow-far 0.5, snow-near 0.4). No flakes.
- snow-coming (forecast snow): the deck and the hush. No flakes.
- rain: the deck, soft rain (rain, a 270 dp tile), and a few irregular slicks clustered in the low ground right
  of the app name, wider and shallower with distance, reflecting amber and rose with a darker damp rim (puddles).
- fog: luminous ground fog lying across the basin floor over the near terrain, so the buttes rise out of it,
  rose near the afterglow and under a third of its strength over the left, where the app name sits (mist).
  The lenticular clouds are hidden.
- overcast: the deck alone.
- freezing-clear: a cobalt lift of clear cold air over both skies (clear-air), broken rime on the far mesa
  tops (frost-far), and silvering of the shadowed ground with broken rime on the ledges and the dune's top and
  sparse soft glints (frost).
- cold-clear: the same at lower strength (clear-air 0.9, frost-far 0.5, frost 0.4).
- hot-clear: a golden haze over the far mesas and the afterglow (haze), drawn beneath the near terrain.

Left out: none of the target pairings. Storm falls back to rain through the sky widening; warm and mild
clear evenings show the plain scene.

Size: about 2.3 MB of variant images. The largest variant (snow) decodes to about 31.6 MB against the
32 MB limit, which is why its snow strips are cropped to the rows they use and its falling layers are
270 dp tiles; add nothing to it without taking something out.
