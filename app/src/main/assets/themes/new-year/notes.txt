New Year is original SVG artwork authored for Hearth. No third-party assets or generated raster imagery are embedded. The scene follows the existing layered landscape system: a navy midnight lake, distant still champagne celebration lights, and a dark shoreline beneath the app dock. The starbursts and shore lights stay steady; only atmospheric haze and broken water reflections move. No year number or personalized text is painted into the scene.

Render with scripts/render-theme.sh new-year. The base is encoded losslessly with the shared fine anti-banding grain. Haze and reflections are authored at half resolution. Haze travels 18 dp per minute with a seamless horizontal wrap. Reflections glide 4 dp each way over 64 seconds and breathe between 0.64 and 0.90 over 90 seconds. Reduce motion uses the renderer's static rest state. The pack is opt-in and does not enter implicit rotation pools.

Weather variants

Original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG sources (10-17),
on the same horizon and in the same palette. No third-party material.
11-bursts.svg copies the two starbursts from 00-lakeside.svg unchanged, so
the champagne lights are drawn above any cloud and stay warm and clear.
Soft gradient layers are authored at quarter size.

- freezing-clear (clear, freezing): a clearer, brighter midnight blue,
  crisp still stars kept away from the clock and the starbursts, rime on
  the near shore crest and a few ice glints at the lake edge.
- snow (snow): a pearl cloud deck with a champagne underside, the starbursts
  above it, snow settling on the near shore, and two soft falling layers
  (60 and 100 dp per 60 s), lighter behind the clock, weather and app name.
- overcast (cloudy, and fog skies by widening): the pearl deck and the
  starbursts above it.

Left out: rain (a winter midnight; rain falls back to overcast) and
hot-clear (not true to the season).
