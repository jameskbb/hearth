Halloween

Original Hearth SVG artwork in the collection's illustrated landscape style.
No generated painting, photographic texture or third-party references are used.
Harvest Evening remains a separate, unchanged scene.

A turning path and three overlapping banks establish the corn maze. Long folded
leaves, irregular tassels and cooler distant rows create depth. Three friendly
carved pumpkins sit against the nearest corn bank. A broad crescent-eyed smile,
a taller open-eyed companion and a little winking grin have distinct carvings.
Small reflected amber shapes connect their steady candlelight to corn and earth.
No angry brows, fangs, horror props or photorealistic detail. The left header,
app-name area and lower dock remain naturally quiet and dark.

00-sky.svg is the still sky and distant hills. 01-maze.svg contains the maze,
path, pumpkins, grounded support posts, sagging cables and steady bulb cores.
02-lights.svg is a half-resolution strip of local amber halos around distant
bulbs. Only that strip moves in brightness: one smooth 180-second cycle from
0.46 to 0.66 opacity, with phase zero. Rise and fall each take 90 seconds.
The cores stay visible at minimum brightness. No flashing, flickering, drifting
lamps, moving corn, introductory fade or pumpkin flicker. Reduce motion draws
the complete scene with midpoint glow; the gallery preview uses that same rest
state. Backgrounding suspends animation through the shared renderer.

Build offline with scripts/render-theme.sh halloween, Chrome and a Python
interpreter with Pillow and numpy. SVGs and theme.json are complete editable
sources. Rendered images use existing lossless WebP and anti-banding grain.
The three layers stay below the 32 MB decoded limit.

The pack remains manual by default, with no recommended dayparts. Use Single
Theme or explicitly add Halloween to a custom schedule or Surprise Me pool.

Weather variants

Original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG sources (10-17),
on the same horizon and in the same palette. No third-party material. Soft
gradient layers are authored at quarter size. The pumpkins' candlelight and
the string lights are never covered: every variant layer sits behind the
maze or parts around the pumpkins, and lights.webp stays on top.

- fog (fog): the moonlight spreads into a wide silver halo that lifts the
  sky; moonlit fog lies in the far hollows behind the maze and thinly over
  the near ground, parting around the pumpkins, and a drift over the far
  end of the maze (17-fog-maze) so its corn banks fade into the mist.
- rain (rain, and storm by widening): the moonlit overcast deck, light
  falling rain (fall: 120 dp per 30 s), a soft sheen at the open end of the
  path and warm candle reflections pooled in front of the pumpkins.
- overcast (cloudy, and snow skies by widening): a pearl cloud deck that
  thins around the crescent, which still shows with a soft halo.

Left out: snow (Halloween evenings are rarely snowy; snow falls back to
overcast), freezing and cold clear, and hot-clear (not true to the scene).
