Easter

Original SVG artwork authored for Hearth, using the established layered
landscape language. No stock images, external references or third-party art.
SVG sources retain the complete editable artwork; shipped WebPs are rendered
through scripts/render-theme.sh and scripts/encode-art.py with fine grain.

Sage and blue hills recede into a warm spring dawn. A blossom tree and softly
colored Easter eggs sit on the right meadow ridge, above the app dock. The
upper corners and shaded sage ridge leave space for the clock, optional Home
label, app name and navigation. The landscape and seasonal details stay still.

Soft pink clouds drift continuously at 21 dp per minute. Transparent matching
edges let the cloud strip wrap without a seam. Reduce motion uses the same
rest composition without a clock. The pack is manual by default, with no
recommended dayparts or implicit automatic rotation.

Muted ink uses E6 alpha. The lower-left meadow ridge is deep sage throughout
the landform, keeping small hints readable without a label backing surface.
