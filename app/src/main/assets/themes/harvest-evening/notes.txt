Harvest Evening

Original Hearth SVG artwork, authored for this pack using the existing Dusk
and Golden Prairie landscape vocabulary. No third-party artwork, fonts, or
reference images are embedded. The palette carries copper autumn foliage,
harvested fields, uncarved pumpkins and a pale gourd into deep slate shadows.
There are no spooky motifs, faces, text, logos, or personal details.

The landscape remains still. A separate half-resolution atmospheric strip
travels continuously at 21.3 dp per minute, using the existing wrapping drift
renderer. Its edges are transparent so the repeat is quiet. There is no
introductory event, flashing, or seasonal clock dependency. Reduce motion
shows the same full scene at rest, and backgrounding stops animation through
the shared renderer. Dark upper corners and the lower landscape leave room
for the existing clock, optional Home label, app names, and controls.

Build offline with scripts/render-theme.sh harvest-evening using a Python
interpreter with Pillow and numpy. The base and opaque landscape use the
existing lossless WebP and anti-banding grain pipeline; the cloud strip keeps
alpha. Source SVGs and theme.json are the reproducible source of every image.

This seasonal pack opts out of implicit automatic rotation and offers no
default daypart recommendations. Choose it in Single Theme or intentionally
add it to a custom schedule or Surprise Me pool.

Weather variants

Original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG sources (10-17),
on the same horizon and in the same palette. No third-party material. Soft
gradient layers (veil-overcast, sky-crisp, fog-far, fog-near) are authored at
quarter size because they hold no detail; the rest are half size.

- frost (clear, freezing): a crisper blue upper sky over the unchanged copper
  sunset, frost silvering the upper faces of the field folds in broken,
  tapering fills (brightest toward the sun), rimed stubble and stems in the
  near field and sparse ice sparkle inside the frosted areas.
- fog (fog): luminous gold fog pooled in the far valley behind the orchard,
  and a thin low fog over the near fields that clears around the pumpkins,
  gourd and tree. Both thin on the left behind the focused app name.
- rain (rain, and storm by widening): the pearl overcast sky, soft falling
  rain (fall: 120 dp per 30 s) and rain-washed glints on the field folds and
  beside the pumpkins.
- overcast (cloudy, and snow skies by widening): a
  pearl cloud deck that stays slate behind the clock and lets the low sun
  glow through, with fuller cloud bands replacing clouds.webp.

Left out: snow and snow-coming (a harvest evening is before the snow),
hot-clear (not true to the season) and cold-clear (frost covers freezing;
a cold clear evening shows the plain scene, which already reads as one).
