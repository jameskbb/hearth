Dusk is the original Hearth scene, split from design/art/dusk.svg (which stays the reference and is not
changed by the weather layers): layer 00 is the sky, sun and ridges, layer 01 the lit wisps.

Weather variants

Provenance: original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG sources; no third-party material.
The ridge-following layers (11, 13, 16, 18) reuse the four ridge paths and the ridge displacement filter
of layer 00, so what they draw sits exactly on the drawn crests. All are evening light over the same
horizon, sun and ridges; the sun and its amber band stay in every variant.

- snow: a lavender-pearl cloud deck (deck) with the sun still glowing in a clear slot under it, snow lying
  above a snow line on every ridge, rose toward the sun and fading downward with no edge line (snow-caps), and two falling-snow layers
  (snowfall-far, snowfall-near) that drift and fall at different speeds. The wisps are hidden.
- snow-coming-frost (forecast snow, freezing): the deck, the pearl-rose hush over the lower sky (hush),
  and a light dusting (snow-caps at 0.45). No flakes.
- snow-coming (forecast snow): the deck and the hush; the hush before snow, no flakes.
- rain: the deck, broken tapering wet light on the crests on the sun side only (glints), and soft falling
  rain (rain).
- fog: luminous valley fog lying between the ridges (fog), rose near the glow, thinner toward the left
  where the app name sits.
- overcast: the deck alone, with the wisps hidden.
- freezing-clear: a cobalt lift of clear cold air over the upper sky (clear-air), a few first stars kept to
  the top 40% of the sky and thinned near the sun (stars), and rime as a broken frosted fill on the top few
  dp of each ridge, warm near the sun and silver-blue away from it, with soft round glints (frost).
- cold-clear: the same at lower strength (clear-air 0.9, stars 0.85, frost 0.5).
- hot-clear: an apricot-gold haze that spreads the sun's bloom and lies mostly above the far ridges, so the
  ridges still step in value (haze).

Left out: none of the target pairings. Storm falls back to rain through the sky widening. Warm and mild
clear evenings show the plain scene, which already is one. Dusk has no buildings, so the warm light kept
in wet and snowy variants is the sun and its band rather than windows.

Size: about 2.13 MB of variant images (deck, snow-caps and fog are the largest). The smooth veils (hush,
clear-air, haze) are drawn at a quarter size, 480 px wide, since they hold only gradients.
