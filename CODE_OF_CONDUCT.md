# Community guidelines

Hearth welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Be respectful, give useful feedback, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Disagree with ideas without attacking people. Harassment, threats, hate
speech, and sharing someone else's private information are not acceptable.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or limit
participation to keep those spaces welcoming and useful.

To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately if a channel is available.
Otherwise, ask for a private contact in an issue without identifying anyone
or repeating sensitive material. Reports will be handled with discretion;
no response time or particular outcome is promised.
