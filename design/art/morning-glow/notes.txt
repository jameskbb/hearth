Morning Glow is original layered artwork created for Hearth. Its image-generation reference was produced with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool from the art direction in the development request. The shipping SVG layers were authored specifically for Hearth and do not embed third-party material.

Weather variants

Provenance: every weather layer is original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG sources (the same horizon, ranges, hills and palette family). No third-party material is embedded. Layer files numbered 10 and up are weather layers; base layers 00-04 are unchanged.

Layers:
- 10-sky-pearl: an opaque half-size sky (data-scale 1) added over the base sky: a pearl and silver cloud deck with the sunrise still breaking through low and warm. Shared by overcast, rain and fog. Clouds thin a little behind the clock.
- 11-sky-snow: an opaque half-size snow sky, lavender pearl overhead and rose-gold where the sun glows through. Shared by snow and both snow-on-the-way looks.
- 12-sky-crisp: an opaque half-size clear sky in sharper air: clean blue, a pale clear band and a tight bright sun. Shared by cold and freezing clear.
- 13-sky-golden: an opaque half-size sky in warm golden haze with a large blooming sun, for hot clear.
- 14-hills-snow: replaces hills.webp on snow mornings (the variant's one full-size replacement). The snow is modelled: rose-gold light on the slopes facing the sunrise, cool lavender-blue in the hollows and on the shaded left, and a periwinkle near hill in shade so the dock still reads.
- 15-peaks-snow: half-size overlay on the ranges: snow on the summits and on the ridge in front, its left side in cool shade so the art behind the focused app name stays mid-toned. At reduced opacity it is the light dusting before freezing snow.
- 16-snowfall, 17-rainfall: sparse, soft, seamless 960x540 tiles for the fall motion. They read as a quiet still texture with Reduce motion. No flashes.
- 18-rain-glints: wet light along the hill crests, brightest toward the sun.
- 19-frost: hoarfrost silvering the grass: a pale blue-green frost settles over the shaded faces and hollows below each crest, strongest away from the sun, while the sun-facing crests keep their green and gold; a few sparkles inside the frosted areas. Full strength for freezing, about a third for cold.
- 20-fog-veil: luminous pearl fog, held still. It thins to under a third over the left of the frame where the focused app name and hint sit. Used three times in the fog look: high in the sky so the overcast turns luminous (its thin part then sits behind the clock), a deep bank over the ranges, and a fainter drift in the hollows of the hills.
- 21-haze-golden: replaces haze.webp on hot clear mornings with a warm golden shimmer, same shapes and motion.

Variants, in match order: snow, rain, fog, snow-coming-freezing (light dusting and frost), snow-coming, freezing-clear, cold-clear, hot-clear, overcast. Every look keeps the sunrise warmth on the right. Overcast also covers storm (widened through rain) and cloudy skies at any temperature. No pairing was left out.
