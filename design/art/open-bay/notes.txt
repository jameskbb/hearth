Open Bay

Original Hearth environment artwork, created for this project with the built-in
image generation tool. No third-party reference images or artwork were used.
The complete generation prompt is in prompt.txt beside these sources.

00-workshop.svg embeds the original PNG painting losslessly. It is intentionally
a raster painting inside an SVG source, rather than a vector approximation of
wood, tools and brushwork. The SVG is self-contained and renders offline through
scripts/render-theme.sh. No image generation service is needed to rebuild it.
The original composition is scaled uniformly and cropped by less than a pixel
at its native size to the 16:9 canvas, then rendered to the 1920x1080 base.

The painting supplies architectural, outdoor, midground and foreground depth.
Its structure stays still to avoid sliding joinery, a moving car, or seams.
01-haze.svg adds a feathered half-resolution wash inside the bay, with a narrow
180-second opacity range. 02-dust.svg adds sparse half-resolution motes in that
light. They drift just six dp over a 240-second cycle, with matching overscan
and a separate 140-second opacity cycle. Both use the shared theme renderer.
At rest they use midpoint opacity; Reduce motion preserves the full painting.

Palette: cool slate and blue-green shadows, walnut and worn metal, restrained
amber daylight. All assets use Hearth's existing SDR/sRGB and grain pipeline.
The upper corners and dark foreground are reserved for Home lettering.
There are no baked-in words, logos, license plates, or personal details.

This pack opts out of implicit automatic rotation. It remains available for
Single Theme and intentional custom Surprise Me or schedule assignments.
