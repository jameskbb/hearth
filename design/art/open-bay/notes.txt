Open Bay

Original Hearth environment artwork, created for this project with the built-in
image generation tool. No third-party reference images or artwork were used.
The complete generation prompt is in prompt.txt beside these sources.

00-workshop.svg embeds the original PNG painting losslessly. It is intentionally
a raster painting inside an SVG source, rather than a vector approximation of
wood, tools and brushwork. The SVG is self-contained and renders offline through
scripts/render-theme.sh. No image generation service is needed to rebuild it.
The original composition is scaled uniformly and cropped by less than a pixel
at its native size to the 16:9 canvas, then rendered to the 1920x1080 base.

The painting supplies architectural, outdoor, midground and foreground depth.
Its structure stays still to avoid sliding joinery, a moving car, or seams.
01-haze.svg adds a feathered half-resolution wash inside the bay, with a narrow
180-second opacity range. 02-dust.svg adds sparse half-resolution motes in that
light. They drift just six dp over a 240-second cycle, with matching overscan
and a separate 140-second opacity cycle. Both use the shared theme renderer.
At rest they use midpoint opacity; Reduce motion preserves the full painting.

Palette: cool slate and blue-green shadows, walnut and worn metal, restrained
amber daylight. All assets use Hearth's existing SDR/sRGB and grain pipeline.
The upper corners and dark foreground are reserved for Home lettering.
There are no baked-in words, logos, license plates, or personal details.

This pack opts out of implicit automatic rotation. It remains available for
Single Theme and intentional custom Surprise Me or schedule assignments.

Weather variants

Open Bay is a workshop interior, so its weather is what shows through the open
bay and the light it throws into the room; the workshop itself stays dry and
warm, and its lamps and amber wood stay. All variant layers are original SVG
work drawn for Hearth. Most are keyed from the painting itself: they draw
00-workshop.svg through SVG filters (luminance or warmth as alpha) and clip
to the bay, so the weather follows its own trees, hills, stone wall and floor
sheen. No third-party material and no image generation. Every layer is a soft
half-resolution overlay; no base layer changed and nothing is replaced.

- overcast (sky cloudy): the sky and far hills in the bay turn pearl silver
  at full strength (keyed from the painting's palest, bluest pixels), the
  garden and stone wall keep their form under a light 22% wash, and the sun
  streak on the floor turns from amber to soft silver.
- rain (sky rain, and storm through the sky widening): the pearl light, long
  soft streaks of varied weight falling past the bay (still, so they read the
  same with motion off; the workshop stays dry), a cool, vertically streaked
  reflection of the doorway on the wet floor below the threshold and small
  glints. The dust motes are hidden for rain.
- snow (sky snow): the pearl light, snow resting on the sunlit tree tops, the
  stone wall and the drive, soft flakes in the doorway and a little drift on
  the threshold. The flakes are still rather than falling: a falling layer
  wraps over the whole canvas and would snow inside the workshop.
- fog (sky fog): the pearl light, the garden fading into luminous warm-silver
  mist (deepest toward the far hills), and a breath of it over the threshold.
- freezing-clear: crisper, bluer air in the bay; the drive, wall tops and near
  grass silvered with frost (shadowed ground lifted toward silver-blue, lit
  tops white) and sparkling in the low sun; feathery rime in the lower
  corners of the small side window, kept faint below the weather line.

Every bay layer is feathered about 10 dp into the jambs and over the
threshold, so no clip edge shows.

Left out, and why:
- snow-coming: under a heavy snow sky the doorway looks the same as overcast,
  so a separate variant would be a copy. Cloudy skies with snow forecast show
  overcast; clear skies with snow forecast show the plain scene.
- cold-clear and hot-clear: the base already is a clear, warm golden
  afternoon, and neither could be told apart from it honestly. Cold and hot
  clear days show the plain scene.

Entries are ordered most specific first: snow, rain, fog, freezing-clear,
overcast.
