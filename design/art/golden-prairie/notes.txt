Golden Prairie is original layered artwork created for Hearth. Its image-generation reference was produced with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool from the art direction in the development request. The shipping SVG layers were authored specifically for Hearth and do not embed third-party material.

Weather variants

All weather art is original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG layers (same mesas, prairie swells and grass tufts); no third-party material. Sources are 10-27 in this folder; each renders at half resolution (data-scale="1").

- rain: pearl sky and silver cloud banks, soft shower veils trailing from the clouds and a faint partial rainbow rising from the left mesas, opposite the low sun, sun on the wet prairie with sheen along each swell and glints of standing water, and sparse soft rain streaks (fall).
- snow: pearl sky under a low deck of cream cloud rolls, the far mesas blue-gray rock with snow on their tops and ledges, fresh snow along the rims of the red rock, swells under snow growing taller toward the viewer, each a lit cream top fading into a lavender lee, dry gold stalks in clusters, the nearest swell in cool shadow with a lit crest and low drifts and the gold tufts rising straight out of it, and soft falling flakes (fall).
- fog: pearl sky with the low sun as a pale disc, luminous fog over the far mesas, and bright tapered ground fog pooled in the troughs between the swells.
- snow-coming-freezing: forecast snow at freezing: the snow-sky deck and veiled sun, pearl haze, silver frost in the lee of each swell and rime on every near tuft.
- snow-coming: forecast snow: the same hushed sky with a light frost tint on the prairie; no flakes.
- overcast: pearl sky, silver cloud banks and pearl haze over the mesas.
- freezing-clear: crisp air (a deeper zenith and a sharp bright horizon band), the shaded lee of each swell silvered with sparse sparkles while the sunlit tops stay gold, rime on the near grass.
- cold-clear: the same crisp air with a lighter touch of frost in the troughs.
- hot-clear: an apricot low sky, thick golden heat haze over the mesas, a shimmer band above the far prairie and the grass bleached toward pale gold.

No pairings are left out. Mild and warm clear skies use the plain scene, which is already a warm afternoon.
