Halloween

Original Hearth SVG artwork, authored for this pack in the same layered
landscape style as Harvest Evening, Dusk and Christmas. No generated painting,
photographic texture or third-party reference image is used.

A crescent moon, blue-green hills and a raised pumpkin patch form a quiet
night scene. Three prominent carved pumpkins have rounded eye openings,
upturned smiles and single square teeth. No angry brows, fangs, horror motifs
or photorealistic detail. Smaller uncarved pumpkins recede into the field.
The dark upper corners and lower foreground leave room for Home lettering.

Terrain and candlelight stay still. A separate half-resolution cloud strip
wraps at 21.3 dp per minute through the existing renderer. Its edges are
transparent. There is no flashing, flicker or introductory event. Reduce
motion shows the same scene at rest and backgrounding stops animation.

Build offline with scripts/render-theme.sh halloween, Chrome, and a Python
interpreter with Pillow and numpy. SVGs and theme.json are the complete
sources. The rendered pack uses existing lossless WebP and anti-banding grain.

Halloween is separate from Harvest Evening, whose original uncarved autumn
scene remains unchanged. The Halloween pack is manual by default, with no
default dayparts; add it to custom schedules or pools only by explicit choice.
