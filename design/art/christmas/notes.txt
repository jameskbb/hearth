Original Hearth artwork, authored as editable SVG layers for the Christmas pack.
No third-party artwork or imagery is used.

The scene extends the existing navy-shadow landscape suite with silver-green
snow banks, a dark evergreen grove, and one Christmas tree with steady amber
lights. The holiday detail stays in the middle-right art area. The top-left
header, upper-right Home label and lower app dock retain quiet dark surroundings.

Cloud and powder strips drift continuously at about 20 dp per minute. Tree,
moon, lights and snow banks stay fixed. There are no flashes or transient bursts.
The shared renderer stops all motion when Hearth leaves the foreground or Reduce
motion is enabled. The rest state remains a complete still landscape.

This is an opt-in occasion pack, excluded from implicit automatic rotation and
recommended day periods. It can be explicitly assigned through Appearance.

Regenerate offline with scripts/render-theme.sh christmas. The source layer
names establish composition order; source and bundled manifests match.


Weather variants

Original work drawn for Hearth from this pack's own SVG sources (10-17),
on the same horizon and in the same palette. No third-party material.
13-snow-trees.svg reuses the grove's own evergreen outlines. Soft gradient
layers are authored at quarter size. The lit Christmas tree and its star
are never covered.

- freezing-clear (clear, freezing): a clearer, brighter night blue with a
  wider moon glow, crisp still stars away from the clock, and ice sparkle on
  the snow field and bank crests.
- snow (snow): falling snow on the existing silver banks. A pearl cloud deck
  lit by the moon, fresh snow resting on every tier of the evergreens and
  along the near bank crests, and two falling layers: many small distant
  flakes (fall: 60 dp per 60 s) and fewer larger near ones (100 dp per
  60 s). Flakes are lighter behind the clock, weather and app name; still,
  under Reduce motion, they read as a quiet snowy texture. The base cloud
  strip is hidden behind the deck.
- overcast (cloudy, and fog skies by widening): the pearl deck alone, with
  the moon glowing through a thinner patch.

Left out: rain (snow and overcast suit the scene better; rain falls back to
overcast) and hot-clear (not true to the season).
