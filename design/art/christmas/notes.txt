Original Hearth artwork, authored as editable SVG layers for the Christmas pack.
No third-party artwork or imagery is used.

The scene extends the existing navy-shadow landscape suite with silver-green
snow banks, a dark evergreen grove, and one Christmas tree with steady amber
lights. The holiday detail stays in the middle-right art area. The top-left
header, upper-right Home label and lower app dock retain quiet dark surroundings.

Cloud and powder strips drift continuously at about 20 dp per minute. Tree,
moon, lights and snow banks stay fixed. There are no flashes or transient bursts.
The shared renderer stops all motion when Hearth leaves the foreground or Reduce
motion is enabled. The rest state remains a complete still landscape.

This is an opt-in occasion pack, excluded from implicit automatic rotation and
recommended day periods. It can be explicitly assigned through Appearance.

Regenerate offline with scripts/render-theme.sh christmas. The source layer
names establish composition order; source and bundled manifests match.
